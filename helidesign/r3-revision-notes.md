# R3 continuous one-piece grip

User-requested revision: remove the notched visual transition between the cylindrical bearing housing and fork cheeks.

The previous version was already one fused solid. R3 replaces the cylindrical outer housing with a 10 x 10 mm housing continuous with the cheek outer faces, and adds 0.8 mm external corner rounds along four continuous rails. No adhesive joints, separate printed parts, or assembly seams are required.

Verified by Fusion: one solid body; volume 1.920642 cm^3; bounding box 10 x 21.5 x 28 mm. Preview visually inspected.

Bearing pocket diameters, axial locations, blade gap, bolt hole and pitch attachment hole are retained from R2. Minimum side wall around a 6.15 mm bearing pocket is now 1.925 mm before local geometric effects, compared with 2.925 mm radial wall in the earlier 12 mm cylindrical housing. This shape change has not been demonstrated to improve structural strength.

All R2 interface assumptions and research limitations remain applicable. This is an unpowered fit prototype; no FEA, flight-strength, fatigue, bearing-retention, or printed-fit validation has been completed. Do not spin this prototype.
