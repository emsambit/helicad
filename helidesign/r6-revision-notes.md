# R6 reference-style grip

Changes: compact 12 mm OD x 8 mm cylindrical outer housing, loft transition to a 10 x 9 mm fork envelope at z=12 mm, two 3 mm thick rounded cheeks separated by a provisional 4 mm gap. Cheek end radius 4.5 mm, bolt center z=25 mm, total length 29.5 mm. Angled integral pitch arm with provisional 1.8 mm mounting hole centered at x=4,y=14 mm.

Oval transverse assembly access through X replaces R5's circular window through Y. Oval width 6.8 mm in Y; ends z=11.6 and 20 mm. Introduce bearings through this oval at z=15.8 mm, shift onto the Z bearing axis, and lower into the stack before fitting the blade. Bearing pocket and stack axial positions remain as documented in R5: inner radial 0..2.5; spacer 2.5..3.9; outer radial 3.9..6.4; washer 6.4..6.5; thrust 6.5..9.3 mm. Inner radial bearing enters from the bottom.

Fusion verification: one solid body, 1.725274 cm^3. Nominal envelope insertion-path sampling found no points inside plastic for all five hardware envelopes (r6-clearance-sampling.json). Sampling is not a continuous collision proof or physical fit test and excludes shaft, retention fasteners, installed hardware and tools.

Reference image used for appearance only; this is not an exact LOGO 200 replacement. Blade root, sleeve fit, bearing tolerances, retention/preload, pitch geometry and printed PETG strength remain unverified. Oval openings leave narrow cheek rails; no structural advantage is claimed. No flight-strength solve has been completed. Use only for unpowered fit checks.

Printable STL and STEP contain the plastic grip only. Metal hardware must be supplied separately.
