# R4 round one-piece grip

Replaces the R3 square housing with one continuous 12 mm diameter cylindrical envelope, 28 mm long. A 4 mm wide slot from z=12 mm upward forms the two integral curved cheeks. The pitch arm and its mounting hole remain. All exported geometry is one solid body, Fusion-reported volume 2.226246 cm^3.

This changes the cheek section to a circular segment rather than a constant 3 mm plate. Top faces are flat; outside surfaces are round. Bearing pockets and axial positions remain as in R2. Blade gap, bearing fit allowance, bolt and pitch interfaces remain provisional. No strength or fit validation is implied by the shape revision. Earlier nominal plate stress calculations do not represent the new cheek geometry.

Preview visually inspected. Unpowered fit prototype only; do not spin at operating RPM.
