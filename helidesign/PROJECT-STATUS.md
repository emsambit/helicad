# Saved helicopter grip project

Current revision: R7, grip-r7-fit-only.f3d (editable Fusion archive).
Other current files: grip-r7-fit-only.step, grip-r7-fit-only.stl, grip-r7-preview.png, create-grip-r7.py.
Earlier revisions and research/check results are retained in this directory.

User targets: 240 g total takeoff mass (supersedes earlier 200 g), 4000 RPM, initially PETG. Alternative manufacturing materials/processes have been discussed but no validated material selection has been finalized.

Status: unpowered fit prototype. Nominal bearing envelope insertion from the bottom was checked. No structural FEA solve or physical fit/flight test has been completed. Model material was last inspected as Fusion default Steel; this does not represent a validated PETG or aluminium material assignment.

Outstanding compatibility issues from the latest supplied OMPHOBBY assembly drawing:
- Actual stepped feathering-shaft journal diameters and lengths remain unknown; drawing labels shaft diameter5 x58 mm, while bearings have3 mm IDs.
- M2x6 retaining-screw engagement, bottoming clearance, tool access and preload are unverified.
- Diameter5 mm retaining washers cannot pass through R7's4.8 mm shoulder opening; final intended washer location and load path require review.
- Relocated internal shoulder and thrust-race contact have not been matched to the stock retention system.
- Blade root/sleeve dimensions and pitch-link attachment geometry remain provisional.

Do not release this design for flight, production tooling, or final machining without resolving those interfaces and validating the structure.

Saved at the user's request. No files were sent to manufacturers or published.
